1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Trois Mousquetaires ("the Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your personal data. It is structured to align with global standards, including the GDPR (EU/UK) and the CCPA as amended by the CPRA (California).

This single policy governs all applications published under the Trois Mousquetaires umbrella (collectively, "the Apps"):

  • NoGrudge (previously named Sub Settle): shared expense tracking and group settlement — ledger entries, balances, and settlement records.
  • Yu > Fu x Kane: chase puzzle game — progress and scores.
  • Quotiv: quote and invoice generator — business, customer, and billing records.
  • Stack Tower: casual stacking game — gameplay progress and scores, stored on your device.
  • Dosewell: medicine reminders, and optional alerts to family — medicines and doses, stored on your phone unless you link phones with family.
  • Tally: receipt scanning, mileage, and expense reports — receipts, receipt photos, and trips, stored on your phone unless you sign in to back them up. Tally is covered in full by Section 20.

Scope of each section — The Apps differ substantially in what they collect. Section 3 lists only the data common to every App. Sections 4 through 8 state what each individual App collects, and are the authoritative list for that App. Where a section applies to only some Apps, it says so.

By using any of the Apps, you acknowledge you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree, please do not download or use them.

4. App-Specific Data: NoGrudge

NoGrudge can be used in two ways: with a Google account, or as a guest. Either way, your account and your data are stored on our servers so that groups stay in sync across devices and members.

Because NoGrudge is collaborative, expenses you add to a group are visible to the other members of that group. Deleting your account does not remove entries other members rely on — see Section 15.

When you use NoGrudge, with a Google account or as a guest, we collect:

  • Account data: your name, email address, and profile picture, as supplied by Google Sign-In. A guest account has no email address or profile picture; you enter your name yourself. We never receive, see, or store your Google password.
  • Your phone number: every account needs one, including guest accounts. We use it so friends can find and add you, and to keep your balances correct.
  • Shared ledger & transaction data: expense titles, descriptions, amounts (in USD or your local currency), timestamps, receipt images, category tags, comments, and the balance calculations between group members.
  • Subscription data: your NoGrudge Pro purchase and renewal status, validated through Google Play Billing. We never receive your card, bank, or payment credentials — see Section 13.
  • Contact details of people you invite: when you pick someone from your address book to invite or add to a group, that person's name and phone number are sent to our servers. Only the person you choose is sent — see Contacts below.
  • Device identifiers: a unique device ID (UUID) and your push-notification token, used to deliver reminders and balance alerts.
  • Sign-in records: the IP address each sign-in session was started from, kept for security and fraud prevention. These records are kept while your account exists and are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Advertising identifier and ad delivery data: see Section 9, which applies to this App. Your ledger entries, receipts, and contact details are NOT passed to the advertising SDK.

Contacts. NoGrudge asks for permission to read your contacts so you can pick a friend to invite or add to a group. We explain why before the system permission prompt appears, and you can decline — the App still works, you just enter the person's details yourself. If you grant it, the App reads the names and phone numbers in your address book on your device to show you the list. When you choose someone, that person's name and phone number are sent to our servers so we can add them to your group. Contacts you do not choose are never sent to us, and we do not upload, copy, or retain your address book.

Receipts, photos, and the camera. Photos are always optional. Free accounts can upload a group photo. Attaching a receipt photo to an expense is a NoGrudge Pro feature. You can pick an existing image through your device's system photo picker, so the App sees only the image you select and never your wider photo library, or you can take a new photo — NoGrudge asks for camera permission the first time you do, and you can decline and use the picker instead. The camera is also used to scan a receipt and read its details: that scan runs entirely on your device, and the scanned image is not uploaded to us. Only an image you choose for a group or attach to an expense is uploaded.

Guest mode. If you choose Sign up as guest, NoGrudge creates an account on our servers without a Google sign-in. Like every account, a guest account needs a name and a phone number. Your groups, expenses, comments, and photos are stored on our servers, the same as for a Google account. Advertising still applies — see Section 9. To delete a guest account and its data, use Account → Delete account in the App. It is deleted in the same way as any other account — see Section 15.

NoGrudge: what we do not collect — NoGrudge does NOT read your device's location, does NOT use an analytics SDK or a crash-reporting SDK, and does NOT access your microphone, calendar, call logs, SMS, or files. Contacts and camera are used only as described above, and both are optional. It never receives your payment card or bank details. Google AdMob, which shows the ads, collects its own data, and may infer your approximate location from your IP address — see Section 9.

8. App-Specific Data: Dosewell

Dosewell is a medicine reminder. It shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK, and never uses your data for advertising. Much of it works only on your phone; what reaches our servers depends on the features you choose.

Using Dosewell on your own. Your medicines, reminder times, and dose history are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us. There is no sign-in and no account.

Just say it. If you add a medicine by speaking, your phone's speech service (on most phones, Google's) turns your words into text. That text is sent to our server, which asks Google's Gemini AI to turn it into a medicine schedule. We do not store the text after the answer is returned. The microphone is used only while Just say it is listening, after you tap it.

Photos of medicine boxes. The camera is used to read a medicine box on your phone. The photo is never uploaded.

Linking phones with family (optional). When you link phones with a code, Dosewell creates an anonymous account for your phone — a random ID; no name, email address, or phone number is required — and keeps the following on our servers so the phones you linked can see it:

  • Health data: your medicines (name, dose, times, and how many are left), the doses due today and tomorrow, and whether each was taken, skipped, or snoozed.
  • Phone health: battery level, whether reminders are allowed to ring, time zone, app version, and when the phone last checked in — so your family is told if reminders can't ring.
  • Names you enter: your name, and the nickname your family uses for you.
  • Device identifiers: a random account ID and your push-notification token, used so alerts reach the linked phones within seconds.
  • Subscription data: whether your family care subscription is active, validated through Google Play Billing. We never receive your card or bank details.

Only phones you linked with a code can see this data. Phone numbers you enter for calling or WhatsApp stay on your phone and are never uploaded. We use health data only to remind you and to show it to the phones you linked — never for advertising or profiling, and we never give it to anyone for their own use. Section 10 gives the legal basis.

Dosewell: what we do not collect — Dosewell does NOT read your location or contacts, does NOT upload photos or voice recordings, does NOT use an advertising identifier, and does NOT use an analytics SDK or a crash-reporting SDK.

Dosewell reminds and records. It is not medical advice and not a medical device. Always follow your doctor.

You can delete your data at any time from within the App using Settings → Delete my data — see Section 15.

15. Retention & Deletion

We keep personal data only as long as necessary.

  • NoGrudge: you can delete your account at any time using Account → Delete account in the App. Deletion is immediate: your name, email address, phone number, and profile photo are removed right away. Shared ledger items in active groups are anonymized ("Deleted User") so the remaining members keep accurate historical balances. Backups are overwritten within 60 days.
  • NoGrudge guest accounts: a guest's data is stored on our servers like any other account. Use Account → Delete account to delete it; it is deleted in the same way as above.
  • NoGrudge activity logs: the activity feed is shown for 30 days on free accounts and for 3 months on NoGrudge Pro. Entries are kept for up to 3 months, then deleted automatically — on a free account, entries older than 30 days are hidden from view but still stored until that point, because a single entry is shared by the free and Pro members of the same group. Only the log entries are removed. The expenses themselves are never deleted by retention — an expense remains until a member deletes or settles it, on both tiers.
  • Yu > Fu x Kane: you can delete your account at any time using the Delete Account control inside the App. This removes the name and email address associated with your Google Sign-In, and your gameplay progress, from our systems within 30 days.
  • Quotiv: you can delete your account at any time using the Delete Account control inside the App. This removes your Google Sign-In identifiers, business profile, and the invoices, quotations, and client records you created, within 30 days. Because tax law may require you to retain your own invoicing records, export your documents before deleting — we cannot recover them afterwards.
  • Quotiv activity logs: the activity feed is kept for 30 days on free accounts and for 3 months on a paid subscription, then deleted automatically. Only the log entries are removed. Your invoices, quotations, and client records are never deleted by retention — they remain until you delete them or close your account, on both tiers.
  • Stack Tower: we hold no Stack Tower data. Logging out removes your Google name, email address, and photo from your device immediately; clearing the App's data or uninstalling it deletes your game progress permanently. Step-by-step instructions for every App are at https://troismousquetaires.com/delete-account
  • Dosewell: if you use it on your own, everything is on your phone — Settings → Delete my data, clearing the App's data, or uninstalling deletes it. If you linked phones, Settings → Delete my data also removes your account and data from our servers immediately. Dose history on our servers is kept for up to 90 days, and an account whose phone has not checked in for 90 days (for example, after uninstalling) is deleted automatically with its data. A family care subscription is not cancelled automatically: cancel it in Google Play.
  • All Apps: backup archives are overwritten within 60 days on our automated cycle. Deleting one App's account does not delete your accounts in the others — to remove all of them, say so in your request.

20. App-Specific Data: Tally

Tally is a receipt and expense tracker. You scan receipts, log mileage, and build expense reports. It works without an account. What reaches our servers depends on whether you sign in and which features you use. This section covers everything specific to Tally, including its ads, legal bases, service providers, retention, and audience. Sections 2, 3, 12, 14, 18, and 19 apply to Tally as they do to every App.

Using Tally without signing in. Your receipts, receipt photos and PDFs, amounts, merchants, notes, categories, payment methods, mileage trips, and reports are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us. To count your free week, the App sends our server a scrambled (one-way hashed) form of your phone's Android ID and the date you first opened the App. We cannot turn it back into the Android ID.

Reading receipts. When you scan a receipt, its text is read on your phone by Google ML Kit. On phones that support it, Google's on-device AI model (Gemini Nano) helps pick out the shop, total, tax, date, and category. Both run on the phone. Your receipt photos and their text are not sent to us or to any cloud AI service to be read.

Finding receipts in your photos (optional, off by default). If you turn on Find receipts in photos and allow access to your photos, Tally checks your recent photos for receipts, also in the background when new photos are added. This happens on your phone, and photos are never uploaded for it. With Selected only access, Tally sees just the photos you pick.

Importing receipt files or a spreadsheet of receipts happens on your phone. Exports and PDF reports are made on your phone and go only where you choose to share them.

Signing in (optional). You can sign in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email address, and you can add a password later. Once you are signed in, backup and sync is on (you can turn Sync off in Settings), and we keep the following on our servers:

  • Account details: your email address, your name (from Google, or as you enter it), your Google account ID if you sign in with Google, a scrambled (hashed) password if you set one, your plan, and when your free week started.
  • Your receipts: merchant, amount, currency, taxes, date, category, payment method name, notes, and whether an expense is reimbursable, with the receipt photos and PDFs.
  • Your mileage trips (the places you type, distance, purpose, and notes), reports, categories, payment method names, and report settings, such as the name, company, and email addresses you enter for reports.
  • Device data: your push-notification token, so we can tell your other phones to sync, and the scrambled device ID used for the free week.
  • Subscription data: whether you are on Tally Pro or Max and when it renews, validated through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We never receive your card or bank details.

Only you can see this data: Tally has no shared or group features. It is stored on our server in the United States (Oregon): the database and your receipt files are on that server, and its nightly backups are kept there too.

Bank statements (Max plan, optional). If you import a bank statement (PDF, CSV, OFX, or QFX), the file is uploaded to our server, which reads the spending lines and turns them into draft receipts. The file is kept with your account until you delete it or your account. Tally does not connect to your bank and never asks for your bank login.

Emailing receipts to Tally (Pro plan, optional). Where this feature is available and you turn it on, Tally gives you a private forwarding address. Email sent to it is received by our email provider, Mailgun, and passed to our server. We keep the receipt attachments (photos and PDFs) as receipts in your account, with the sender's address and the subject line. We do not keep the rest of the email. When a receipt arrives, a notification showing the subject line is sent to your phone.

Exchange rates. When you ask for an exchange rate, our server looks up that day's rate from a public exchange-rate service. Only the currencies and the date are sent.

Tally: what we do not collect — Tally does NOT read your location, contacts, microphone, SMS, or call logs, and does NOT use an analytics SDK or a crash-reporting SDK. The Help improve tally switches in Settings send nothing today. Tally never asks for your bank login or card number.

Tally keeps records. It is not tax, accounting, or financial advice.

Tally: ads and your choices. Every new user gets a free week with everything unlocked and no ads. After it, Tally stays free, and the Free plan may show a full-screen (interstitial) ad from Google AdMob after a scan. Tally Pro and Max show no ads. The ads SDK starts only when an ad is about to be shown. To select and measure ads, Google collects your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (from which approximate location may be inferred), device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Your receipts, amounts, and photos are NOT passed to the advertising SDK. Google acts as an independent controller for this processing, under the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
