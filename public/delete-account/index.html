Delete your account and data

For all apps published on Google Play by Zohaib Shahzad Trios / Trois Mousquetaires: Yu Fu Kane, Brick Tower: Stack & Build (Stack Tower) and Sub Settle · Last updated October 2026

Before you start

Each app has its own account, so deleting one does not delete the others. Find your app below and follow its steps. If you cannot open the app, email contact@troismousquetaires.com from the email address linked to your account, name the app, and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

Yu Fu Kane

  • Step 1: Open Yu Fu Kane and go to Settings.
  • Step 2: Scroll to Danger Zone and tap Delete account, then confirm.

What is deleted: your account, the name and email address from your Google Sign-In, and your saved game progress, both on our servers and on your device. This happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What is kept: nothing linked to you. Server backups are overwritten within 60 days.

Brick Tower: Stack & Build (Stack Tower)

Stack Tower has no servers of its own. Your Google name, email address and photo, and all your game progress, are stored only on your device.

  • Step 1: Open Stack Tower and tap your profile picture (or initial) at the top of the home screen.
  • Step 2: Tap Logout. Your Google name, email address, photo and sign-in token are removed from the device immediately.
  • Step 3 (to also delete game progress): uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Stack Tower → Storage → Clear data. Scores, coins and progress are deleted permanently.

What is kept: nothing. We hold no copy of your account or game data.

Sub Settle

  • Step 1: Open Sub Settle and go to Account.
  • Step 2: Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. You will be asked to confirm twice, and the deletion happens immediately.

What is deleted: your name, email address, phone number and profile photo; your login; and the notification tokens for your devices. Your account can no longer be signed in to.

What is kept, and why: expenses you shared with other people stay visible to those people, shown against "Deleted user". They are part of their records, and removing your half of a shared expense would change what your friends owe each other. Money you owed or were owed is not cancelled by deleting your account. A Sub Settle Pro subscription is not cancelled automatically: cancel it in Google Play first.

How long it takes: deletion in the app is immediate. Backups are overwritten within 60 days.

Advertising data (all apps)

Ads in our apps are served by Google AdMob, which processes your device's advertising ID under Google's own terms. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
