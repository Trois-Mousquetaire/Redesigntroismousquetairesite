1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Trois Mousquetaires ("the Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your personal data. It is structured to align with global standards, including the GDPR (EU/UK) and the CCPA as amended by the CPRA (California).

This single policy governs all applications published under the Trois Mousquetaires umbrella (collectively, "the Apps"):

  • Sub Settle: shared expense tracking and group settlement — ledger entries, balances, and settlement records.
  • Yu > Fu x Kane: chase puzzle game — progress and scores.
  • Quotiv: quote and invoice generator — business, customer, and billing records.
  • Stack Tower: casual stacking game — gameplay progress and scores, stored on your device.

Scope of each section — The Apps differ substantially in what they collect. Section 3 lists only the data common to every App. Sections 4 through 7 state what each individual App collects, and are the authoritative list for that App. Where a section applies to only some Apps, it says so.

By using any of the Apps, you acknowledge you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree, please do not download or use them.

3. Information We Collect Across All Apps

The following applies to every App:

  • Technical data necessary to deliver the App: IP address, device model, and operating system version. This is processed to serve the App, maintain security, and diagnose faults.
  • Support correspondence: messages, attachments, and contact details you provide when you contact us.

Scope of the App-specific sections — Everything else is App-specific and is listed in Sections 4 through 7. We do not operate a single cross-App profile of you. Accounts and progress are kept separate per App unless you explicitly link them.

4. App-Specific Data: Sub Settle

Sub Settle can be used in two ways. With a Google account, your data is stored on our servers so that groups stay in sync across devices and members. In Guest mode the App runs entirely on your device and sends us nothing at all.

Because Sub Settle is collaborative, expenses you add to a group are visible to the other members of that group. Deleting your account does not remove entries other members rely on — see Section 14.

When you sign in with a Google account, we collect:

  • Account data: your name, email address, and profile picture, as supplied by Google Sign-In. We never receive, see, or store your Google password.
  • Shared ledger & transaction data: expense titles, descriptions, amounts (in USD or your local currency), timestamps, receipt images, category tags, comments, and the balance calculations between group members.
  • Subscription data: your Sub Settle Pro purchase and renewal status, validated through Google Play Billing. We never receive your card, bank, or payment credentials — see Section 12.
  • Contact details of people you invite: when you pick someone from your address book to invite or add to a group, that person's name and phone number are sent to our servers. Only the person you choose is sent — see Contacts below.
  • Device identifiers: a unique device ID (UUID) and your push-notification token, used to deliver reminders and balance alerts.
  • Advertising identifier and ad delivery data: see Section 8, which applies to this App. Your ledger entries, receipts, and contact details are NOT passed to the advertising SDK.

Contacts. Sub Settle asks for permission to read your contacts so you can pick a friend to invite or add to a group. We explain why before the system permission prompt appears, and you can decline — the App still works, you just enter the person's details yourself. If you grant it, the App reads the names and phone numbers in your address book on your device to show you the list. When you choose someone, that person's name and phone number are sent to our servers so we can add them to your group. Contacts you do not choose are never sent to us, and we do not upload, copy, or retain your address book.

Receipts, photos, and the camera. Attaching a receipt is a Sub Settle Pro feature and is always optional; free accounts upload no images at all. You can pick an existing image through your device's system photo picker, so the App sees only the image you select and never your wider photo library, or you can take a new photo — Sub Settle asks for camera permission the first time you do, and you can decline and use the picker instead. The camera is also used to scan a receipt and read its details: that scan runs entirely on your device, and the scanned image is not uploaded to us. Only an image you choose to attach to an expense is uploaded.

Guest mode. If you choose Continue as Guest, Sub Settle creates no account and transmits nothing to our servers. Your groups, expenses, and receipt images are stored only in the App's local storage on your device. We cannot see this data, cannot recover it for you, and do not back it up. Advertising still applies — see Section 8. If you later sign in with a Google account, you will be asked whether to upload your local data; if you decline, it stays on the device. To erase Guest data, use Clear local data in Settings or uninstall the App.

Sub Settle: what we do not collect — Sub Settle does NOT collect precise or approximate location from your device, does NOT integrate a crash-reporting or analytics SDK, and does NOT access your microphone, calendar, call logs, SMS, or files. Contacts and camera are used only as described above, and both are optional. It never receives your payment card or bank details.

5. App-Specific Data: Yu > Fu x Kane

Yu > Fu x Kane is a chase puzzle game.

  • Account data: your name and email address, received from Google when you sign in with Google Sign-In. Authentication is handled entirely by Google — we do NOT collect or store a password. We do NOT collect a phone number or a profile avatar.
  • Gameplay data: level progress, scores, and in-game settings.
  • Advertising identifier and ad delivery data: your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or IDFA on iOS where permitted), IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, collected by Google AdMob to serve interstitial and rewarded ads. See Section 8.

Yu > Fu x Kane: what we do not collect — Yu > Fu x Kane does NOT use push notifications, does NOT integrate a crash-reporting SDK, and does NOT collect contacts, location, camera, or microphone data.

You can delete your account at any time from within the App using the Delete Account control — see Section 14.

6. App-Specific Data: Quotiv

Quotiv is an invoicing and quotation tool for freelancers and small businesses.

  • Account data: your name and email address, received from Google when you sign in with Google Sign-In. Authentication is handled entirely by Google — we do NOT collect or store a password.
  • Business profile data: your business name, address, logo, tax or VAT registration number, payment terms, and any bank or payment details you choose to display on your documents.
  • Client and document data: the names, addresses, email addresses, and other contact details of the clients you invoice, together with line items, descriptions, amounts, currencies, dates, and the invoices and quotations you generate.
  • Subscription data: subscription status, purchase tokens, and renewal state, validated through the app store. We do NOT receive or store your payment card details.
  • Advertising identifier and ad delivery data: see Section 8, which applies to this App.

Quotiv: data about your clients — The client details you enter are personal data about other people, who are not our users. For that data you are the controller and we act as your processor — we store and process it only to provide the App to you, and never for our own purposes. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to enter your clients' information and for telling them how it is used. A Data Processing Agreement is available on request from contact@troismousquetaires.com.

You can delete your account at any time from within the App using the Delete Account control — see Section 14.

7. App-Specific Data: Stack Tower

Stack Tower is a casual stacking game with no in-app purchases.

Stack Tower has no servers of its own. Apart from advertising data handled by Google AdMob, everything below is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

  • Account data (optional): your name, email address, and profile photo, received from Google when you sign in with Google Sign-In, and kept on your device to show your profile. Authentication is handled entirely by Google — we do NOT collect or store a password. You can play as a guest without signing in.
  • Gameplay data: scores, high scores, coins, XP, streaks, missions, achievements, and in-game settings.
  • Advertising identifier and ad delivery data: see Section 8, which applies to this App.

8. Advertising & Advertising Identifiers

This section applies to all four Apps — Sub Settle, Yu > Fu x Kane, Quotiv, and Stack Tower.

Each App displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. In Yu > Fu x Kane these are interstitial and rewarded formats. To select and measure those ads, Google collects and processes:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or IDFA on iOS where you permit it).
  • Your IP address, from which approximate location may be inferred.
  • Device and app information, and your interactions with ads shown to you.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID at Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • iOS: you can turn off tracking for the App at Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Advertising: consent and personalization — Google acts as an independent controller for the processing described above. Its handling of the data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and by Google's advertising terms.

Where you consent, ads may be personalized using your advertising identifier — a practice the CCPA/CPRA classifies as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. Section 10 explains your opt-out rights.

In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Sub Settle and Quotiv present a consent prompt on first launch through Google's User Messaging Platform. If you decline, you will continue to see ads, but they will be non-personalized. Yu > Fu x Kane and Stack Tower do not currently present this prompt; instead, they request only non-personalized ads, for every user in every region.

We do not store your advertising identifier on our own servers, and we do not link it to your account data.

Under GDPR Article 6, we process your data under these lawful bases:

  • Consent: for personalized advertising in Sub Settle and Quotiv, for analytics cookies on this website, and for marketing messages. We never rely on consent for users we treat as children — they are served non-personalized ads only, as set out in Section 15. You may withdraw consent at any time, and withdrawal is as easy as giving it.
  • Legitimate interests: to detect fraud, ensure security, debug stability, and improve the experience.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with tax, anti-money-laundering, or legal disclosure requirements.

10. California Disclosures (CCPA / CPRA)

This section applies to California residents:

  • Categories collected in the past 12 months: identifiers (including an advertising identifier), commercial information, and internet or other electronic network activity.
  • No sale: we do NOT sell your personal information, and we receive no money for it in any App.
  • Sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising: in every App, where you have not opted out and are not a user we treat as a child, your advertising identifier is shared with Google AdMob to serve personalized ads. Under the CPRA this constitutes "sharing."
  • Your right to opt out: you may opt out of this sharing by resetting or deleting your advertising identifier using the device controls in Section 8, by declining personalization in the in-app consent prompt where one is presented (Sub Settle and Quotiv), or by emailing contact@troismousquetaires.com. We honour Global Privacy Control signals where they reach us.
  • Sensitive information: processed only for necessary operations, never to infer characteristics about you. Because we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics, the right to limit its use does not arise — you may still contact us to confirm.
  • Categories of sources: you (what you enter in an App), your device, Google Sign-In, the app stores, and Google AdMob.
  • Business and commercial purposes: providing and maintaining the Apps, processing subscriptions, serving and measuring advertising, security and fraud prevention, debugging, and meeting legal obligations.
  • Categories of third parties we disclose to: cloud hosting providers, Google (Sign-In, AdMob, Play Billing), Apple (App Store billing), and push-notification providers. Section 12 names each one.
  • Retention: we keep each category only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for. Section 14 gives the periods and the deletion routes.
  • No cross-App profiling: we do not merge data across Sub Settle, Yu > Fu x Kane, Quotiv, and Stack Tower to build advertising profiles.

12. Data Sharing & Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We share it only with the following, under Data Processing Agreements where they act as processors:

  • Cloud infrastructure: Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud (hosting and secure storage).
  • Google Sign-In: authentication for Sub Settle, Yu > Fu x Kane, Quotiv, and Stack Tower. In Stack Tower, the details Google provides stay on your device.
  • Google AdMob: advertising delivery and measurement in all four Apps. Google acts as an independent controller for this processing, not as our processor.
  • App store billing: Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase, for Sub Settle Pro and Quotiv subscription validation and renewal status.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management. It receives your purchase status and an app user ID so we can tell which plan you are on across your devices. It does not receive your name, email, ledger, or documents, and never your card or bank details.
  • Push notifications (Sub Settle only): Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service.

Sub Settle does not connect to, integrate with, or move money through any payment provider. Recording a settlement in the App marks a balance as paid between members; no funds are transferred by us or through us.

13. International Transfers

Your information may be stored on servers outside your state or country. For EU/UK data transferred to the United States, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, together with supplementary technical measures where required.

We are based in Pakistan, which the European Commission has not recognised as providing an adequate level of data protection. Our staff access personal data from there in order to operate and support the Apps, which is itself an international transfer. That transfer relies on Standard Contractual Clauses, and on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum for UK data, with access limited to the staff whose role requires it.

14. Retention & Deletion

We keep personal data only as long as necessary.

  • Sub Settle: you can delete your account at any time using the Delete Account control in Settings. Profile identifiers (name, email) are permanently purged or anonymized within 30 days. Shared ledger items in active groups are anonymized ("Deleted User") so the remaining members keep accurate historical balances.
  • Sub Settle Guest mode: no data ever reaches us, so there is nothing for us to delete. Use Clear local data in Settings, or uninstall the App, to remove everything from your device.
  • Sub Settle activity logs: the activity feed is shown for 30 days on free accounts and for 3 months on Sub Settle Pro. Entries are kept for up to 3 months, then deleted automatically — on a free account, entries older than 30 days are hidden from view but still stored until that point, because a single entry is shared by the free and Pro members of the same group. Only the log entries are removed. The expenses themselves are never deleted by retention — an expense remains until a member deletes or settles it, on both tiers.
  • Yu > Fu x Kane: you can delete your account at any time using the Delete Account control inside the App. This removes the name and email address associated with your Google Sign-In, and your gameplay progress, from our systems within 30 days.
  • Quotiv: you can delete your account at any time using the Delete Account control inside the App. This removes your Google Sign-In identifiers, business profile, and the invoices, quotations, and client records you created, within 30 days. Because tax law may require you to retain your own invoicing records, export your documents before deleting — we cannot recover them afterwards.
  • Quotiv activity logs: the activity feed is kept for 30 days on free accounts and for 3 months on a paid subscription, then deleted automatically. Only the log entries are removed. Your invoices, quotations, and client records are never deleted by retention — they remain until you delete them or close your account, on both tiers.
  • Stack Tower: we hold no Stack Tower data. Logging out removes your Google name, email address, and photo from your device immediately; clearing the App's data or uninstalling it deletes your game progress permanently. Step-by-step instructions for every App are at https://troismousquetaires.com/delete-account
  • All Apps: backup archives are overwritten within 60 days on our automated cycle. Deleting one App's account does not delete your accounts in the others — to remove all of them, say so in your request.

For users we treat as children, the following applies:

  • We do NOT use personal data for behavioral advertising, profiling, or cross-context tracking.
  • We collect no more personal data than is needed to run the App.
  • Parents and guardians may request to review the personal data we hold about their child, have it deleted, and refuse any further collection, by writing to contact@troismousquetaires.com.

18. This Website, Cookies & Analytics

Sections 1 to 17 describe the Apps. This section covers troismousquetaires.com itself, which collects far less.

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are read and how people arrive. It sets cookies in your browser and collects your IP address (from which approximate location may be inferred), the pages you view, and basic device and browser information. IP anonymization is enabled.

  • Nothing is loaded until you agree. On your first visit a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Google Analytics is not fetched, and no analytics cookie is set, unless you choose Accept.
  • Declining costs you nothing. The site works identically either way, and we do not ask again on every page.
  • Changing your mind: use the Cookie preferences link in the footer to reopen the banner and switch your answer at any time.
  • Legal basis: your consent, under GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and ePrivacy rules on storing information on your device. Withdrawing it is as easy as giving it.

Google processes this analytics data under the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not use advertising cookies on this website, we do not build advertising profiles from it, and this data is kept separate from any App account you may have.

Reading this Privacy Policy, or any other page, never requires an account and never requires accepting cookies.
